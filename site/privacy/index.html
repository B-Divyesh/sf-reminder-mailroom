Plain-language policy

Privacy

Effective 30 August 2026

Reminder Mailroom keeps mail processing on your computer. We do not operate a service that receives or stores mailbox content.

What stays on your device

Mailbox settings, sorting rules, PDF hashes, thread keys, and audit history stay locally. Passwords and OAuth tokens use your operating system credential manager. Email content is read only when it matches your rule. It travels directly between your computer and configured mail servers.

License verification

Mailroom Plus sends only your license token and product slug to the Sociobot license API. A validity result is cached locally for one day. Sociobot/Dodo operates checkout as merchant of record.

Website data

This site loads no analytics, ads, tracking pixels, or third-party fonts. It fetches public release metadata from the GitHub API. A restored license token stays in browser storage.

Sample demo

The demo uses the separate demo:reminder-mailroom browser key. Resetting or leaving the demo removes that sample state. It never connects to a mailbox.

Your control

You can export the audit as CSV. Removing the app data directory clears settings, rules, hashes, and history. Remove credential entries separately to clear secrets.

Contact

Email privacy questions to privacy@sociobot.in.
