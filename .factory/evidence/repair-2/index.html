How it works

Route invoice PDFs without changing senders

Mailroom is not an invoice maker or a new bookkeeping system. It handles the one routing decision other tools blur.

  1. 01

    Name the mail you expect

    Set a subject term, optional sender, and mailbox. Nothing else is inspected.

  2. 02

    Preview matches

    See which PDF would become canonical before Mailroom forwards anything.

  3. 03

    Archive once

    The first PDF hash and invoice thread are recorded locally. Reminders remain untouched and duplicates stay out.

Private desktop processing

Mail connects directly from your computer

Mail travels directly between your configured IMAP and SMTP servers. Credentials use the operating system keychain. Rules, hashes, and audit history stay on your device. There is no account, mailbox upload, telemetry, or behavioral analytics.
